# Hellhound Derby: art credits

The sprites in `hounds/`, `crowd/` and `props/` are rendered from these models (in Blender: see `art/derby/` in the
Hexcast repository). The same list is the Credits card at the bottom of the Derby tab. This file is written by
`art/derby/scripts/export_assets.py` from `art/derby/credits.json` and `art/derby/props.json`.

- **The hounds**: "Hell Hound" by Adam (shadow798), CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hell-hound-36f3f384fd9b41a9a8dae1aaf22349d3
  Posed into a gallop, recoloured per hound and rendered to sprites. Three-Heads Terry's extra heads are copies of its head.

- **Hungry Harry's skull**: "Dog (Canis familiaris) Dolichcephalic" by The University of Edinburgh (CT scan courtesy of Dr. Tobias Schwarz, prepared by Brian Mather), CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/5d8d86a270b64f05aab45c3d09f350a6
  Decimated, re-oriented and given a moving lower jaw. The rest of Harry's bones were made for Hexcast.

  https://www.mixamo.com
  Posed for Hexcast (idle, cheer, fist pump, jeer, clap, the throw) and rendered to sprites. Only the frames ship, never the character files.
