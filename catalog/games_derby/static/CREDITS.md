# Hellhound Derby: art credits

The hound sprite sheets in `hounds/` are rendered from these models (Blender, see `art/derby/` in the
Hexcast repository). Both are licensed **CC BY 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- **"Hell Hound"** by Adam (**shadow798**) on Sketchfab:
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/hell-hound-36f3f384fd9b41a9a8dae1aaf22349d3
  Changes: posed into a procedural gallop, recoloured per hound, and rendered to 2D sprite frames.
  Three-Heads Terry's extra heads are copies of the model's own head.

- **"Dog (Canis familiaris) Dolichcephalic"** by **The University of Edinburgh** (Open.Ed; CT scan
  courtesy of Dr. Tobias Schwarz, prepared by Brian Mather) on Sketchfab:
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/5d8d86a270b64f05aab45c3d09f350a6
  Changes: decimated, re-oriented and given a moving lower jaw; it is Hungry Harry's skull.

The monsters in the stands (`crowd/`) are **Mixamo** characters (Adobe, https://www.mixamo.com):
Warrok W Kurniawan, Maw J Laygo, Mutant, Goblin D Shareyko, Nightshade J Friedrich, Parasite L Starkie,
Pumpkinhulk L Shaw, Vampire A Lusth, Warzombie F Pedroso, Yaku J Ignite and two of the numbered characters
(Ch25, Ch30). Adobe lets them be used royalty free in personal, commercial and non-profit projects; only
rendered frames ship here, never the character files. Their moves (idle, cheer, fist pump, jeer, clap, the
throw) were posed for Hexcast, not taken from Mixamo's animations.

Everything else was made for Hexcast: Hungry Harry's other bones, the effects (fire, slime, lightning,
sulfur, flies, soul fire), the track, the stands, the thrown junk, the sounds and the dialogue.
