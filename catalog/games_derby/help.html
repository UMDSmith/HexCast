Hellhound Derby /games#derby

Hellhounds race on one track on the games overlay (/games/overlay, or ?game=derby), and chat bets on the winner. Each race starts with a lineup of up to 6 hounds, each wearing a race number, with its odds and its form. A bet is a number and an amount. If that hound wins, the bet pays its multiplier; any other result loses the stake. All bets are against the bank (your bot), through the shared ledger. The odds come from the form book (every hound's recent results), so a hound on a hot streak is the favourite and pays less. When the bets close, the server draws the winner with exactly the chances shown (a cryptographic RNG). The tumbles, bites, slime, flaming arses, thrown junk and trash talk are only dressing: they can't change the result. A very close finish gets a photo finish. Now and then a guest racer takes a hound's place (guest_chance); your own guests are a local pack in config/games_derby_guests/ (never part of the add-on: copy the folder to race them on another Hexcast). Placement, look, the title (your branding) and the hounds' language live in the Games panel's Edit Mode. Details: docs/derby.md.

GET|POST /games/api/derby/start{seconds, test} — open a lineup (seconds: the bet window) → the field (number, hound, chance, multiplier); test writes nothing to the ledger or the form book; 409 while a race runs
GET|POST /games/api/derby/bet{user, amount, number} → mult, pays, debits, player; number may also be the hound's name; the same hound again adds; 409 bets_closed outside the lineup
GET|POST /games/api/derby/remove{user, number?} — take back what went down in this window (refund)
GET|POST /games/api/derby/nextend the current phase now (close the bets, finish the race)
GET /games/api/derby/table · user/{name} · ledger · history · bets · validatethe STATE · one player · this game's ledger events · finished races + stats · rules, the odds model, the field and the form book · dry-run a bet
GET /games/api/derby/form · POST /form/clearthe form book (races, wins, last places, streak) · reset it
GET /games/api/derby/guests · /guests/file/{name}the guest racers the overlay can draw (the add-on's and your local pack's) · a local pack's sprite sheet
GET|POST /games/api/derby/stopend the game: a race already drawn counts (winners are paid), an open window is refunded
GET|POST /games/api/derby/preview{overrides, seconds} — Edit Mode's Test in OBS: the scene on screen for a few seconds with that look (STATE.preview); never touches the game · /preview/clear
