Craps /games#craps

A bank-craps table on the games overlay (/games/overlay, or ?game=craps for its own source). Chat bets hexcoins; bets stay on the table across rolls like a casino. The server rolls two dice with secrets.SystemRandom, the overlay throws them onto exactly those faces, the stickman calls it, the puck moves and every bet settles by standard casino rules. Nothing can force the dice. Hexcast holds no balances: the bank is Hex, whose hexbank takes the coins for every debit and pays every credit in a numbered, persisted ledger — or Hex does the math itself and only tells the overlay what to show (Mode B, below). All endpoints return {"ok": true/false, ...}.

GET|POST /games/api/craps/rollthrow the dice (aliases /spin, /play) — returns the dice, settlements (every bet with action), credits per user, summary, table (+ the roll's ledger events with wait); 409 {"error": "busy", "retry_in_ms", "state"} while a roll is in the air, showing its result, or in cooldown
GET|POST /games/api/craps/bet{user, bet, amount, target?} or {"bets": [...]} (up to 200) → accepted, rejected (each with an error), debits [{user, amount, bet_id, seq}], table. 200 if any were accepted, 400 if all were rejected, 409 bets_closed while the dice are in the air
GET|POST /games/api/craps/remove{bet_id} · {user, bet} ("bet": "odds" takes down just the odds) · {user, all: true} → removed, credits (refunds), ledger; contract bets answer 400, 409 bets_closed while the dice are in the air
GET /games/api/craps/tablephase, point, shooter, hand rolls, every bet, exposure per user, bets_open, auto_roll_in_ms (the countdown — auto-roll or /timer), last_seq
GET /games/api/craps/user/{name}one viewer's bets, exposure and craps session debits / credits / net
GET /games/api/craps/ledger?since=0&limit=500craps' ledger events with seq > since, oldest first (limit up to 5000), plus last_seq, oldest_seq and truncated (the limit cut it, or older events are only in config/games_ledger.jsonl). The ledger is shared with roulette's table (one seq numbering, each event names its game), so gaps are normal; GET /games/api/ledger has every game's
GET|POST /games/api/craps/clearrefund every bet (reason refund), back to a come-out, shooter cleared, countdown stopped; 409 bets_closed while the dice are in the air
GET|POST /games/api/craps/timer · timer/cancelthe roll timer: start (or restart) a countdown now — seconds 5–300 (default bet window), with or without bets, auto-roll on or off; at 0 the shooter's dice go by themselves → {auto_in_ms, table, state}, 409 busy unless idle · stop any countdown → {cancelled, table, state}. Hooking up the bank (hexcoins)

Placing a bet — either (a) check the viewer's hexbank balance → POST /bet → debit each accepted debits[] entry (if a debit then fails, /remove that bet), or (b) debit first → POST /bet → credit back whatever was rejected. Rejected bets and a 409 move nothing. Paying out — tail /ledger?since=<last applied seq>, apply each event once in seq order, and save that seq in Hex together with the hexbank change, so payouts happen exactly once across restarts of either side. Skip the debits Hex already took from a /bet reply; take any other debit (a bet placed from the panel). The tail also catches panel rolls, auto-rolls and timer rolls, take-downs (remove) and clears (refund); running roulette's table too, tail /games/api/ledger (every game) once instead. The /roll?wait=true reply's credits is for announcing in chat, not for paying. The table and ledger persist in config/; seq keeps counting across restarts, and a restart while the dice are in the air cancels that roll (bets stay, nothing is paid). Winnings round down to whole coins — suggest amounts that pay exactly (place 6/8 in 6s, place 4/5/9/10 in 5s, odds on 6/8 in 5s). Step-by-step guide for Hex's developer, chat-command flow and sample code: docs/craps.md.

Hex does the math (Mode B)

The other way to run the money: Hexcast's table stays empty and Hex keeps the bets in its own code. Take the stake in the hexbank and mirror the bets with POST /board → close bets and POST /roll with wait: true and no bets → settle Hex's bets from result (event, total, dice, phase_before, point_before) → credit the hexbank → POST /announce the payouts with the roll's id as spin_id and post the updated /board. The dice, puck and shooter are still Hexcast's fair roll — Hex can't send dice. Never also /bet those bets (they'd be settled and in the ledger twice); auto-roll only counts bets on Hexcast's table, so Hex rolls — or starts the roll timer (/timer) and reads the roll it throws from /last; re-post the board after a Hexcast restart (it's memory only). Recipe and sample code: docs/craps.md.
