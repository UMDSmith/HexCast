Blackjack /games#blackjack

A half-moon felt table on the games overlay (/games/overlay, or ?game=blackjack) with Hex's skeleton hand dealing, a shoe and a discard tray. Up to 14 chatters sit down (the rest wait in a queue) and play standard Vegas blackjack against the bank (your bot) — hit, stand, double, split, insurance, surrender — all through the shared ledger: a bet, a double, a split and insurance are debits, a payout is a credit. Everybody plays at the same time: each action round is a short window (12 s, then 8 s) in which every hand sends one choice; the server then deals them out in seat order, and no choice means stand. The shoe (2–8 decks by how many are seated) is shuffled by the server with a cryptographic RNG and the overlay never gets a card that isn't turned over. A seat is kept between hands only if its player bets again (rebet). The rules (dealer S17/H17, blackjack 3:2 / 6:5, double, splits, insurance, surrender …) are settings and the house edge is computed from them. Placement, look and the plaque's title (title, your branding) live in the Games panel's Edit Mode. Details: docs/blackjack.md.

GET|POST /games/api/blackjack/start{seconds, seats, test} — open the table; test writes nothing to the ledger; 409 while one is open
GET|POST /games/api/blackjack/bet{user, amount, seat?, balance?} — the ante: takes a seat (the first free one, or seat), else the queue; more on a stake adds to it → debits, player; 409 bets_closed outside the betting window
GET|POST /games/api/blackjack/rebet{user} (alias /ditto) — the same ante as last hand
GET|POST /games/api/blackjack/action{user, action: hit|stand|double|split|surrender|insurance|decline, hand?, balance?} — one choice per hand per round, final; double / split / insurance debit at once; 409 actions_closed · shortcuts /hit /stand /double /split /surrender /insurance
GET|POST /games/api/blackjack/remove · leave{user, amount?} — take back (part of) the stake before the deal (refund) · {user} — get up (alias /cashout): stake back before the deal; during a hand it stands and leaves after the settle
GET|POST /games/api/blackjack/next · closeend the current window now · close the table after this hand
GET /games/api/blackjack/table · seats · user/{name} · ledger · history · betsthe STATE · seats + queue · one player (hands and can[], what they may do now) · this game's ledger events · finished tables + stats · rules, payouts, house edge, deck table
GET|POST /games/api/blackjack/stopend the table now: every stake still on the line is returned (a hand in progress is a no-contest)
GET|POST /games/api/blackjack/preview{overrides, seconds} — Edit Mode's Test in OBS: the table on screen for a few seconds with that look (STATE.preview); never touches the game · /preview/clear
