Giveaways /giveaways

Prizes with a picture and a description; a draw from who is in your Twitch chat right now; a countdown with a jumble of names on the overlay (/giveaways/overlay, a 1920×1080 browser source), the winner revealed with confetti, then a chat message for them. Who can win: everyone in chat, followers (optionally for at least some minutes / hours / days) and/or subscribers - any ticked group counts, and every viewer who qualifies has the same chance. The broadcaster, Hexcast's own accounts, the names in Never win and (if ticked) past winners are left out.

"In chat" is Twitch's own list of who is connected to the chat - lurkers too, not people watching with chat closed - and it can lag a minute or two. Followers are checked as names are drawn (so a big chat costs a few lookups); subscribers can only be seen by the channel's own account. It needs the shared Twitch connection signed in with the moderator:read:chatters scope (an account signed in before Giveaways existed: connect it again), and user:write:chat for the chat message.

Each prize has a quantity - one fewer with every winner - and optional private keys or codes: one goes to each winner, shown only in the winners list (and GET /giveaways/api/config), never on the overlay. Reroll draws again for that prize without the winner, who gives the prize and code back.

GET /giveaways/api/statuswhat the overlay shows (show.state: idle · showing · drawing · revealed), the prizes without their codes, who can win, the latest winners, and problem when a draw cannot work
POST /giveaways/api/draw{"prize": "id", "channel"?: "name", "eligible"?: {"everyone": false, "followers": true, "follow_seconds": 86400, "subs": true}} — draw now and start the show; the winner comes back at once, the overlay reveals them after the countdown
POST /giveaways/api/rerolldraw again for the last winner's prize ({"entry": "id"} for an earlier one)
POST /giveaways/api/cancel · hidestop a draw that is counting down (nobody wins) · take the prize or winner off the overlay
POST /giveaways/api/show{"prize": "id"} — show the prize on the overlay, up for grabs
POST /giveaways/api/test{"prize"?: "id", "where": "stream|preview"} — the whole show with made-up names; nothing is recorded or posted
GET /giveaways/api/poolhow many are in chat and could win right now
POST /giveaways/api/prizes · /prizes/{id} · /prizes/{id}/deleteadd ({"name", "description", "image", "quantity", "codes"}), change some fields of, or remove a prize
GET · POST /giveaways/api/configevery setting (countdown, hold, announce, message, sounds, look, exclusions ...) and the prizes; what you leave out stays
GET · POST /giveaways/api/imagesthe prize picture library (POST a file; /images/delete with {"name"})
WS /giveaways/wslive status, {"type": "event", "event": "winner", ...} when a winner shows, and every call above by name: {"cmd": "draw", "prize": "id", "rid": 1}
