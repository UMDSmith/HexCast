Hexfall /games#hexfall

A hex-themed Plinko on the games overlay (/games/overlay, or ?game=hexfall). Each drop has a betting window where chat puts hexcoins up against the bank (your bot), through the shared ledger; then one glowing skull falls through a pyramid of hexagonal pegs. The server flips a fair coin at every peg (left or right, secrets.SystemRandom) and the overlay only animates that path. The skull lands in a slot that pays stake × its multiplier (x0 is a bust); every slot's odds, the table's RTP and the house edge are computed from the binomial probabilities and shown. 3 drops a game, 30 s to bet on the first, 20 s between drops; no bets ends the game. Placement, look and the header's title (title, your branding) live in the Games panel's Edit Mode. Details: docs/hexfall.md.

GET|POST /games/api/hexfall/start{drops, rows, risk, multipliers, seconds, test} — start a game; test writes nothing to the ledger; 409 while one runs; 400 when multipliers does not have rows + 1 numbers
GET|POST /games/api/hexfall/bet{user, amount} → debits, player; betting again adds to this drop's bet; 409 bets_closed outside a betting window
GET|POST /games/api/hexfall/remove{user} — take back this window's bet (refund)
GET|POST /games/api/hexfall/nextend the current phase now (alias /drop)
GET /games/api/hexfall/table · user/{name} · ledger · history · betsthe STATE · one player · this game's ledger events (payout credits) · finished games + stats · the slot table with every slot's multiplier, exact odds, the RTP and house edge
GET|POST /games/api/hexfall/stopend the game: a skull already falling counts (its slot pays), open bets are refunded
GET|POST /games/api/hexfall/preview{overrides, seconds} — Edit Mode's Test in OBS: the scene on screen for a few seconds with that look (STATE.preview); never touches the game · /preview/clear
