Bot-Free Redeems /redeems

Plays a sound from your Soundboard when a viewer redeems a channel point reward, types a chat command like !airhorn, or cheers bits - straight from Twitch to the Soundboard, with no bot or AI in between. Everything is set up in the Redeems tab. It uses the shared Twitch connection, so it works with or without the Twitch plugin.

Setting it up

1. The Soundboard overlay in OBS. Every sound plays on the Soundboard overlay, the same one the Twitch alerts and the games use. If you do not have it yet, add a Browser source in OBS with the URL http://localhost:4747/overlay (use the Hexcast PC's address instead of localhost when OBS runs on another PC), size 1920 × 1080. Untick Shutdown source when not visible and tick Control audio via OBS, so its sound shows in the OBS mixer and goes to your stream.

2. Your sounds. Open the Soundboard tab and drop in sound files (mp3, wav, ogg...) or short videos and GIFs. Each clip's name is what you pick in the Redeems tab. Its volume, trim, position and cooldown are set on the Soundboard and apply to redeems too.

3. Twitch. In the Twitch connection box at the top of the Redeems tab, add your channel name and save. That is all chat commands and bits need: Hexcast reads your chat without signing in. Channel point redeems also need a sign-in as the channel itself (Connect a Twitch account while signed in to Twitch as your channel): Twitch tells only a channel's own account about its redeems. The first time, the box walks you through making a Twitch app (about two minutes). Sign in from a browser on the Hexcast PC: Twitch sends the sign-in back to http://localhost (it takes plain http for no other address), so a tab opened as 192.168... or from another computer cannot finish it. Channel points are only for Affiliate and Partner channels.

4. Your rewards, on Twitch. Make each reward on Twitch as usual: Creator Dashboard → Viewer Rewards → Channel Points → Manage Rewards & Challenges → Add New Custom Reward. Give it a name and a cost. Set its cooldown and limits (per stream, per viewer) there too: Twitch checks them before the points are spent, so a viewer is never charged for a sound that is held back. Leave Require Viewer to Enter Text off for a plain sound.

5. The rows. In the Redeems tab press + Add a redeem sound, + Add a command or + Add a bits sound, fill in the row, pick its sound and press Save. ▶ Test plays the sound on the overlay right away (it is on stream). Then try it for real: redeem the reward, or type the command in your chat.

Channel point redeems

Pick the reward from the list (Hexcast asks Twitch for your rewards; ↻ Reload rewards after you add one on Twitch) and the sound. A row remembers the reward itself, so renaming it on Twitch keeps it working; a reward deleted and made again with the same name is found by its name. When Hexcast cannot list your rewards (not signed in as the channel, or not an Affiliate) you can type the reward's exact name instead. A redeem always plays - there is no cooldown here, because the viewer has already spent the points; use the reward's own cooldown on Twitch.

Chat commands

A chat line that starts with the command plays the sound: !airhorn, or !airhorn and some words. Capitals do not matter, and a row can have a few commands (!airhorn !horn). Typing airhorn in the box saves it as !airhorn.

Who can use it: everyone; subscribers, VIPs and mods; VIPs and mods; moderators; or only you. Cooldown (30 seconds to begin with) holds the command for everyone after it plays; Same viewer again after holds it for the viewer who used it (0 = off). You, the broadcaster, are never held up, so you can test on stream. A command that is on cooldown or not allowed does nothing in chat - the Recent list says why.

Bits

A cheer of at least the amount plays the sound. With several amounts the biggest one the cheer reaches wins: with 100 and 1000 set, 500 bits plays the 100 sound and 1500 bits the 1000 one. A cheer smaller than every amount plays nothing. Bits come from your chat, so they need no sign-in either.

Several sounds on one row

+ another sound adds more sounds to a row: each time, one of them plays at random (never the same one twice in a row). Handy for a "random fart noise" reward.

The Recent list

The bottom of the tab lists the last 40 redeems, commands, cheers and tests, newest first: who, what, and what happened - played (and which sound), or why not: no sound set for that reward, on cooldown, only for subscribers, the clip is not on the Soundboard, the Soundboard overlay is not open... Every row also says ready or what stops it, and how many times it has played.

With the Twitch plugin's alerts

The Twitch tab's alerts can play one Soundboard clip for every redeem and one for every cheer. When a redeem or a cheer has a sound here, only this sound plays: the alert still shows, but its own clip stays quiet for it. Rewards with no row here still play the alert's clip. Switching Bot-Free Redeems off (Sounds on) gives the alerts their clips back.

Several channels

Watching more than one channel (the Twitch connection box takes up to five), every row gets a Channel setting: the primary channel (the default), every channel, or one of them.

When a sound does not play

Look at the Recent list first, then the row's own line.

Nothing shows in Recent for a redeem. Hexcast did not hear about it. The redeem card in the tab says why when it knows (not signed in as the channel, Twitch refused the redeem events). Sign in as the channel's own account in the Twitch connection box; an account signed in long ago may lack a permission, so Connect a Twitch account again as that user. With the Twitch plugin installed, its Status tab lists every Twitch subscription and why one failed.

Recent says "nobody heard it". The Soundboard overlay is not open: check the Browser source in OBS (step 1), and that Shutdown source when not visible is off.

Recent says "played" but there is no sound on stream. The overlay plays it, OBS does not pass it on: tick Control audio via OBS on the source and check its volume in the OBS mixer.

"no sound set for this reward". No row matches that reward: pick it in a row (or check the name you typed), and press Save.

"still in its Soundboard cooldown". The clip has a cooldown of its own on the Soundboard. Lower it there, or use the reward's cooldown on Twitch instead.

The reward list says Affiliate or Partner. Twitch has channel points only for those channels; chat commands and bits work for everyone.

A command does nothing. It has to be the first word of the line. Check Who can use it and the cooldowns (Recent says which one held it).
