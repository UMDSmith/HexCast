How to set it up

Bot-Free Redeems plays a sound from your Soundboard when a viewer redeems a channel point reward, types a chat command like !airhorn, or cheers bits - straight from Twitch, with no bot or AI in between. A row can also answer in chat: !discord posts your Discord address. Five steps, once:

  1. Put the Soundboard overlay in OBS. Every sound plays there. Add a Browser source with this URL, 1920 × 1080, untick Shutdown source when not visible and tick Control audio via OBS (so you can set its volume in the OBS mixer). You only do this once, and the Soundboard tab, the Twitch alerts and the games use the same source.
    http://localhost:4747/overlay
  2. Add your sounds to the Soundboard. Open the Soundboard tab and drop in sound files (mp3, wav, ogg...) or short videos and GIFs. A clip's name is what you pick here; its volume, trim and cooldown are set on the Soundboard and apply here too.
  3. Connect Twitch in the box below: add your channel name. That is all chat commands and bits need. Channel point redeems also need you to sign in as the channel itself (Connect a Twitch account), because Twitch only tells a channel's own account about its redeems - and channel points are only for Affiliate and Partner channels. A row that says something in chat needs a sign-in too (any account - the line is posted as that account).
  4. Make your rewards on Twitch (for redeems): Creator Dashboard → Viewer Rewards → Channel Points → Manage Rewards & Challenges → Add New Custom Reward. Set the cost there, and the cooldown and limits too - Twitch checks them before the points are spent, so a viewer is never charged for a sound that does not play. Skip Require Viewer to Enter Text for a plain sound.
  5. Add a row below for each reward, command or bits amount, pick its sound - or type a chat message, or both - and press Save. Press ▶ Test to hear it on stream, then try it for real from your chat. The Recent list at the bottom shows every redeem, command and cheer, and why one did not play.

Using the Twitch tab's alerts too? A reward or bits amount with a sound here plays only this sound - the alert's own Soundboard clip stays quiet for it, so nothing plays twice. The help page has the details and fixes for common problems.

Bot-Free Redeems

The switch for everything below. Off, nothing plays and nothing is said in chat (handy for a quiet stream), and the Twitch alerts play their own clips again.

Channel point redeems

Pick a reward and the sound it plays, the line it says in chat, or both. A redeem always plays: its cost, cooldown and limits are the reward's own settings on Twitch.

Chat commands

A chat line that starts with the command plays the sound - !airhorn, or several on one row (!airhorn !horn) - or answers in chat: !discord with your Discord address in Say in chat and no sound. Choose who may use it; the cooldown holds it for everyone and the second one for the same viewer. You, the broadcaster, are never held up, so you can test.

Bits

A cheer of at least this many bits plays the sound (and says the chat line, if the row has one). With several amounts the biggest one it reaches wins: with 100 and 1000 set, a 500-bit cheer plays the 100 sound.

Recent

The last redeems, commands and cheers, newest first, and what each one did.
