Twitch connection shared

The sign-in, the channels and the live connection to Twitch that every Twitch module uses - Twitch (chat and alert overlays), Chat Timers and Giveaways. It has no tab of its own: it is installed with whichever of them comes first, and each of their tabs shows the same Twitch connection box, so you set it up once. Your Twitch app, the accounts you signed in with and the channels are the same everywhere.

GET /twitch/api/connectionthe channels (connected how, as whom, live or not), the accounts signed in and the scopes each still lacks, the log - never a secret or a token
POST /twitch/api/connection{"channels": [{"login": "name", "label": "beta", "enabled": true}]} — the channels to watch (up to 5, the first is the primary); only the channels that changed reconnect
POST /twitch/api/chat/send{"text": "...", "channel": "name"} — a chat line as the account Hexcast acts as in that channel (the primary when channel is left out). 400 with error when Twitch would not take it
POST /twitch/api/credentials{"client_id": "...", "client_secret": "..."} — your Twitch app
POST /twitch/auth/logout{"login": "name"} signs that account out; no body signs out all
POST /twitch/api/reconnectreconnect every channel

Accounts and channels

A channel uses the account with its own name if you signed in as it (all its alerts, chat as the broadcaster, its subscriber list), else your default account (the primary channel's own, else the first one signed in) - which then needs to be a moderator there for anything beyond reading chat. With nobody signed in, chat is read anonymously. Channels are independent: editing one, or signing an account in or out, reconnects only the channels it concerns.

Scopes

Hexcast asks Twitch for everything its modules use when you sign in. An account signed in before a module needed something new (moderator:read:chatters for Giveaways, say) is listed with what it lacks: press Connect a Twitch account again as that user to allow it.
