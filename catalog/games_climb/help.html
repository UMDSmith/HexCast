Soul Climb /games#climb

A little damned soul climbs out of a hell pit on the games overlay (/games/overlay, or ?game=climb), and chat bets on how high he gets: a bet is a height from 1 to the top of the pit and an amount. If his best height reaches (or passes) the guess the bet pays its multiplier, if he falls first the stake is lost; the top of the pit is the escape, the jackpot. All against the bank (your bot), through the shared ledger. Up to 5 bets per player per climb, 30 s to bet, 1-3 climbs a game; no bets ends the game. The server rolls every climb with a cryptographic RNG from a published survival model (every height's chance and multiplier: GET /games/api/climb/bets); the demons, bats, near falls and funny falls are only dressing and can't change the outcome. Placement, look and the header's title (title, your branding) live in the Games panel's Edit Mode. Details: docs/climb.md.

GET|POST /games/api/climb/start{soul, climbs, seconds, test} — start a game (soul: a name, or several separated by commas: one per climb); test writes nothing to the ledger; 409 while one runs
GET|POST /games/api/climb/bet{user, amount, height} → mult, pays, debits, player; the same height again adds; 409 bets_closed outside a betting window
GET|POST /games/api/climb/remove{user, height?} — take back what went down in this window (refund)
GET|POST /games/api/climb/nextend the current phase now (close the bets, finish the climb)
GET /games/api/climb/table · user/{name} · ledger · history · bets · validatethe STATE · one player · this game's ledger events · finished games + stats · rules + the table of every height (chance, multiplier) · dry-run a bet
GET|POST /games/api/climb/stopend the game: a climb already decided counts (winners are paid), an open window is refunded
GET|POST /games/api/climb/preview{overrides, seconds} — Edit Mode's Test in OBS: the scene on screen for a few seconds with that look (STATE.preview); never touches the game · /preview/clear
