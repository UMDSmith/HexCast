Set up Live2D

Avatars draws Live2D models with Live2D's own Cubism Core and an open-source renderer built on Live2D's Cubism Framework. They are Live2D Inc.'s software, so they don't ship with Hexcast: read and accept Live2D's terms, and Hexcast downloads them once (about 1.3 MB) into config/avatar_runtime.

What you agree to - Live2D's licenses, not Hexcast's:
  • Live2D Proprietary Software License Agreement (the Cubism Core)
  • Live2D Open Software License Agreement (the Cubism Framework inside the renderer)
Read them yourself - the agreement is between you and Live2D. Publishers of apps that load users' own models need Live2D's publication license. Your models' own terms (from their artist or rigger) apply too.

Library

Live2D models (Cubism 3, 4 and 5 - a folder with a .model3.json). A VTube Studio model folder works as it is: its parameter setup, hotkey expressions and idle animation come along. Kept in media/avatars/models/.

PNGtubers too: a few pictures (quiet, talking, blinking ... - named idle.png, talk.png, blink.png, happy_talk.png ... or anything, and set on its PNGtuber tab), or a layered PNGTuber Plus avatar (its .save file).

Items: a png / jpg / webp / gif, a folder of numbered frames (name_1.png, name_2.png ...) or a Live2D item. Kept in media/avatars/items/.
