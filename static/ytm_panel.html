Connect to YouTube Music Desktop

HexCast reads what's playing from the desktop app's companion server. Nothing leaves your machine.

  1. Open YouTube Music Desktop → Settings → Integrations.
  2. Turn on Companion Server.
  3. Turn on Enable companion authorization — it only stays on long enough to pair.
  4. Click Pair below, then approve the code in the app within 30 seconds.

Extras

Every track change gets POSTed to the forward URL as JSON. The clip name fires a HexCast clip when the song changes — leave blank for silence.

Music folder

Point HexCast at a folder of audio files. It's indexed in the background, so browsing and search stay fast even for tens of thousands of tracks. Nothing is copied or modified; tags & cover art are read on demand with ffmpeg.

Artwork

YouTube Music plays either a real music video or an "art track" — an auto-generated audio upload with a still image. Art tracks cannot be embedded. But YouTube Music pairs most songs with a video counterpart, so the default setting shows that video even while the app plays the audio version. Flipping the app's own Song/Video toggle works too. Videos whose owner blocks embedding fall back to album art on their own.

Input level — should move when audio is playing. If it stays at zero, the tap is wrong.
