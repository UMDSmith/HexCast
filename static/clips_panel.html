Add clips

Paste a Twitch clip/VOD URL, a YouTube link, or a whole blob of chat spam - every Twitch and YouTube link in it is picked out (?t= start timestamps are honoured), duplicates skipped. A single URL from any other site yt-dlp understands (Kick, Streamable, X, a direct .mp4...) works too.

Queue

Drag rows to reorder. Nothing auto-advances - every clip is fired by hand (or by a bot via its #number). Played clips stay in the list, dimmed, until you clear them.

Queue is empty.

Browser source

Add as a Browser source in OBS, sized and placed where clips should appear (the video letterboxes to fit). It renders nothing at all while idle, so it can cover the whole canvas. Uncheck "Shutdown source when not visible".

Playback

Everything streams straight from the source by default - nothing is stored. Pre-download is optional: it fetches clips/videos to media/clips/ on add for instant starts that survive expiring media URLs (files are deleted with their queue item; VODs always stream). The embed fallback covers Twitch and YouTube only; the Twitch embed needs the overlay loaded via a hostname Twitch accepts as parent (localhost works) and can't report when it ends.
